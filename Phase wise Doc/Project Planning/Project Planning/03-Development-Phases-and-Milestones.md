# Development Phases and Milestones

This is a documentation-oriented summary of the observed architecture, not a claim about exact historical dates.

1. **Foundation:** create the React/Vite client and Express API, configure MongoDB and environment variables.
2. **Authentication:** registration/login/logout, HTTP-only cookie handling, current-user endpoint and role checks.
3. **Movie discovery:** movie catalogue, movie detail and showtime discovery endpoints.
4. **Theatre and seat layout:** theatre/show retrieval and seat-map representation.
5. **Booking reliability:** seat holds, hold expiry, booking states and atomic seat updates.
6. **Demo payment:** simulated payment outcomes and idempotency support.
7. **Admin workflows:** catalogue, show, user, dashboard and booking-management APIs.
8. **Deployment:** frontend/backend deployment and environment-variable configuration.
9. **Documentation and validation:** phase-wise documents, test cases, screenshots, and project report.
